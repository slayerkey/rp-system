# Local source baseline recovery, 2026-09-20

This recovery was copied byte-for-byte from the owner PC during the full PackRat source audit. No product behavior, manifest UUID, action UUID, or version was edited. Generated dependency folders, logs, caches, build output, and marketing output were excluded. The newest identifiable package for each missing source UUID is retained under `packages/` with its original SHA-256 in `preserved-packages.json`. `product_matrix.json` is the complete UUID-level inventory.

The original dirty local factory was not committed directly. Product trees copied to their normal repository paths are listed in `copied-projects.json`; standalone historical projects are retained under `external/` with their original paths recorded in `external-projects.json`.
