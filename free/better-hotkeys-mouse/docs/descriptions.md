Hold a key down for real sustained input, or toggle it and walk away. Click and toggle mouse buttons too, on Windows or Mac.

Better Hotkeys & Mouse adds the one thing Stream Deck's stock Hotkey action can't do: hold a key down. Press and hold for sustained input, or toggle it and leave it held while you do other things. Also sends mouse clicks and toggles mouse buttons, which stock Stream Deck cannot do at all.

*Hotkeys that actually hold.*

**Hold Key** - Hold one or more keys for exactly as long as you hold the deck key. Multiple keys together, so Shift + W is real sustained auto-run.
**Toggle Key** - Press once to hold a key down, press again to release it. The key face shows on or off.
**Click Mouse** - Left, right or middle click, wherever your cursor already is or at a spot you set. Stock Stream Deck can't send mouse buttons at all.
**Toggle Mouse Button** - Press once to hold a mouse button down, press again to let go. Handy for a rotate-lock you don't want to keep a finger on.

Every action ships with a live property inspector: record a key by pressing it, or capture a mouse position by moving the cursor and tapping the deck key. Want more? Better Hotkeys & Mouse Pro adds mouse movement, radial select, auto-repeat, drag, scroll and dial support in one plugin.
