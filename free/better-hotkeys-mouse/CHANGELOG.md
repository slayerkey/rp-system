## v0.2.1.0 - 2026-08-04

- Every action's settings panel now shows what Better Hotkeys & Mouse Pro adds, with a
  link that opens the listing in your browser.
- No change to how any action works.

## v0.2.0.0 - 2026-07-29

- This plugin now focuses on the four core actions: Hold Key, Toggle Key, Click Mouse
  and Toggle Mouse Button.
- Move Mouse, Hold Mouse Button, Radial Select, Auto-Repeat, Mouse Drag, Scroll,
  Encoder Hotkey and the mic actions have moved to Better Hotkeys & Mouse Pro.
- If you use any of those actions, install Better Hotkeys & Mouse Pro to keep them.

## v0.1.0.0 - 2026-07-15

- First release.
- Hold Key: keeps one or more keys down for exactly as long as you hold the deck key.
- Toggle Key: press once to hold keys down, press again to release them.
- Click Mouse, Move Mouse, Hold and Toggle Mouse Button, Mouse Drag and Scroll.
- Radial Select for in-game wheel menus, Auto-Repeat, and Encoder Hotkey for dials.
- Mute Mic, Push to Talk and Mic Volume on Windows.
