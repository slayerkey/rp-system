Measured power, calculated energy

Metrics are calculated only from the selected iCUE power sensor.

The primary sensor alone drives session energy, daily energy, history and cost. Comparison sensors stay separate. Pro does not add CPU and GPU sensors together and call the result whole-PC power.

Estimated cost is integrated primary-sensor kWh multiplied by your configured electricity rate. Missing or disconnected intervals are never filled in.

Idle versus active classification is intentionally not claimed in V1 because iCUE does not expose a universal workload-state signal that would make that label reliable across PCs.
