## v1.0.0 - 2026-07-31

- First release. Three pages: interface panels on the home screen, your full 12 slot action bar on its own page, and Wowhead, Raider.IO, Warcraft Logs and patch note links on the Guide page.
- Included for Stream Deck MK.2 and Stream Deck XL, on Windows and Mac.
- Keys match WoW's own default bindings, so there is nothing to set up in game. Park your hearthstone and mount on an action bar slot and the deck runs them.
