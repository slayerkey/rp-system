One league's games, one key

Tap shows the next game. Hold to open that game on ESPN. A hold always does the opposite of a tap, so swapping the setting below swaps both.

Every game on the league's current slate, one at a time, live games first, with its position in the list shown in the corner. In season that is today's fixtures; out of season it is the next match day, so anything not yet started shows its date as well as its time. This is a list of games, not the league table: for where a team sits, use a Standings key. It costs no extra updates, since it reads the same data your team keys already do.
