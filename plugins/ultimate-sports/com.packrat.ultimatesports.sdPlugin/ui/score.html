Which team is this key watching?

Tap opens the game on ESPN. Hold to refresh instead. A hold always does the opposite of a tap, so swapping the setting below swaps both.

The key shows the live score while the game is on, and the countdown to the next matchup when it is not. It updates on its own. A green frame means your team just scored or the game started.

An amber bar along the bottom means the score could not be refreshed, so you are seeing the last one it got, with its age in the corner.
