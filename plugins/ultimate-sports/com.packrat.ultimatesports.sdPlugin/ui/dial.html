Which sports should the dial cover?

Leave everything unticked to follow wherever your teams are. Tick a sport to include its whole board even if you follow nobody in it.

Turn the dial to move through every game across the sports you picked, live ones first. The touch strip shows the matchup and the clock. Press or tap to open that game's page.
