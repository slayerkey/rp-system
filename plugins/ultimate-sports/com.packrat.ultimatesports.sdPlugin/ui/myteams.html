Which teams do you follow?

This list is shared by every key in this plugin, so you only build it once.

    Tap shows the next team. Hold to open that game on ESPN. A hold always does the opposite of a tap, so swapping the setting below swaps both.

    One key holds every team on the list. It shows whatever is most urgent first: a live game, then anything about to start, then the rest of today, then finals. Press to walk through the rest.

    A green frame means a score just changed. An amber bar along the bottom means the score could not be refreshed, so you are seeing the last one it got.

    Not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, team or governing body. Team names and colors identify the teams whose scores you chose to follow.
