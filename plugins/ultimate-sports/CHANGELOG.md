## v1.0.0.0 - 2026-08-21

- First release. Windows and macOS, every Stream Deck model.
- Eleven sports in one plugin: football, basketball, hockey, baseball, soccer, college football
  and college basketball, WNBA, UFC, NASCAR and Formula 1. Nineteen leagues in total, including
  nine soccer competitions, so one deck can follow the Premier League and LaLiga at the same time.
- My Teams: pick your teams once, across as many sports as you like. One key holds the whole list
  and puts the most urgent first, a live game before one about to start before the rest.
- Team Score, Standings and League Games keys work for any team or league in any of the sports.
- Fight cards walk bout by bout with winners filling in. Race weekends give the running order and
  the championship table, one driver per press.
- Stream Deck +: turn the dial through every game across every sport you follow, with the matchup
  and the clock on the touch strip. Press or tap to open that game.
- Hold any key to open that game's page in your browser. A tap does the everyday thing.
- A green frame flashes the moment a score changes.
- Keeps showing the last score it got when the connection drops, with an amber bar along the
  bottom of the key and the age of the data, so a stale number never passes for a live one.
