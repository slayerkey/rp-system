Lite vs Pro

Lite is for short keyboard macros: up to 30 seconds and 60 keyboard events.

Macro Recorder Pro adds mouse recording, 10-minute macros, playback speed, repeat modes, a reusable Macro Library, full editing and import/export. The direct upgrade link can be added after the Pro Marketplace listing is live.

Local by design

Macro contents are never uploaded to PackRat. Lite stores the macro on the local Stream Deck Play action. Do not deliberately record passwords. Windows may block replay into apps running at a higher Administrator integrity level.
