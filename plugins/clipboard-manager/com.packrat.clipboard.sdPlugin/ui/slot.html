Which copy does this key hold?

Give four keys slots 1 to 4 and you have your last four copies laid out in order. Slot 1 is whatever you copied most recently, so the row shifts along as you copy.

Pasting straight in drops the text into whatever window you are working in. Putting it back on the clipboard leaves you to paste it yourself, which is handy when the window you want is not the one in front.

The key shows a shortened preview, but a press always gives you the whole thing.

That is all four slots.

Text you copy is kept on this machine so the slots survive a restart. Images and copied files are skipped.
