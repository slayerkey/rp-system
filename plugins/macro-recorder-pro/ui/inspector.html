What this is for

Macro Recorder is for a timed sequence you perform once and want Stream Deck to remember. Better Hotkeys is the simpler tool for individual keyboard or mouse actions.

Macro contents stay on this PC. Do not deliberately record passwords. Windows may block replay into apps running at a higher Administrator integrity level.
