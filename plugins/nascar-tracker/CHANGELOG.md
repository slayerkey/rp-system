## v1.0.0.0 - 2026-07-30

- First release. Windows and macOS.
- Next Race: the next race and a countdown once it is close.
- Race Field: the running order, one driver at a time.
- Driver Standings: the championship table, one driver at a time with points. Press to walk down
  the order.
- Keeps showing the last data it got when the connection drops, with an amber bar so nothing stale
  passes for current.
