## v1.0.0.0 - 2026-07-29

- First release. Windows and macOS.
- Team Score: live score for the team you pick, refreshing on its own while the game is on.
  When there is no game, the same key shows the next matchup and its tip-off time.
- Standings: your team's rank, record, and games behind. Press the key to swap between the
  conference table and the division table.
- Put a Team Score key down for every team you follow.
- Keeps showing the last score it got when the connection drops, with an amber bar along the
  bottom of the key so you know it is not current.
