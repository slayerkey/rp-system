# Changelog

## 1.0.0.0 - 2026-08-22

First release.

- Plan Value: your usage priced at token rates against what your plan costs. Green once the
  plan has paid for itself.
- Cost: today, 7 days or 30 days, with an optional monthly budget bar that turns red when you
  go over.
- Breakdown: top three by model or by project. Press the key to swap.
- Cache Hit: what share of your prompt came from cache, and what that discount was worth.
- Trend: one bar per day, up to 30 days.
- On a Stream Deck +, the dial scrubs between periods.
- Reads the session files Claude Code and Codex already save on this computer. No account, no
  key, no network call.
