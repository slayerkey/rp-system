Which team is this key watching?

The key shows the live score while the game is on, and the countdown to the next matchup when it is not. It updates on its own. A green frame means your team just scored or the game tipped off.

An amber bar along the bottom means the score could not be refreshed, so you are seeing the last one it got, with its age in the corner.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by any league or club or any team. Team names and colors identify the teams whose scores you chose to follow.
